B20 Inspector

Who controls this B20 token?

B20 is Base's native token standard. Issuers can hold roles to mint, seize, pause and blocklist. Paste a token address to see which of those powers exist, who holds them, and what they mean for your balance.

Independent, read-only tool. Not affiliated with Base or Coinbase. Reads public RPCs straight from your browser; nothing is stored.
